What are the Google Ads Policies?

✅ Google Ads Policies – What You Need to Know (Display Ads)

Google has rules to make sure ads are safe, honest, and user-friendly.
All ads we design follow these rules to help avoid disapproval and delays.

🔹 What Your Ad Can Include
✔ Clear and honest messaging
✔ Professional images or graphics
✔ Simple call-to-action like “Learn More” or “Shop Now”
✔ Content that matches your website or landing page

🔹 What Google Does NOT Allow
❌ Misleading claims or false promises
❌ Fake buttons or system alerts
❌ Low-quality or blurry images
❌ Overcrowded text or confusing layouts
❌ Content related to scams, adult material, or illegal services

🔹 Animated Ads (GIF / HTML5)
✔ Smooth, clean animations
✔ Easy-to-read text
✔ No flashing or aggressive movement

❌ No auto-sound or music
❌ No fake close buttons
❌ No distracting effects

🔹 Ad Size & Quality
Your ads must:
✔ Use correct Google Ad sizes
✔ Be clear on desktop & mobile
✔ Load properly and smoothly

We make sure all designs meet Google’s technical and quality standards.
🔹 Landing Page Matters Too
Google checks:
✔ If your ad message matches your website
✔ If the page is safe and easy to use

Even a perfect design can be rejected if the website doesn’t match — we’ll guide you if needed.
